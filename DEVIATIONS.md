| 2026-11-25 | **Regime-Switching Primary Workload** | Within-Plan Refinement | Homogeneous traces have near-zero switching headroom by construction. Elevated Markov-modulated `regime_switching` (480 s) to primary switching workload while retaining all four §2.2 regimes (`stable`, `bursty`, `alternating`, `shifted`) as controls. |
| 2026-11-25 | **Fair Model Calibration & Paraphrases** | Within-Plan Pilot Fix | Eliminated information asymmetry against zero-shot models via monotone pilot-percentile scale context, full factual policy descriptions, 4 pre-registered neutral paraphrases, and Zhao et al.
